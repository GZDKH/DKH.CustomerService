## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Non-recursive independent owner authorization
Recipient access SHALL preserve Customer's actual active account/profile locks and independent current contact/consent checks. The Delivery callback SHALL authorize from its native accepted actor/duty/assignment and original named human without re-entering Customer. Physical execution checks SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Current self actor
- **WHEN** the genuine owner callback returns its accepted self actor
- **THEN** its profile SHALL equal Customer's actually locked current actor profile before disclosure or receipt
- **AND** a different profile, missing actor, service actor or malformed native actor SHALL deny

#### Scenario: Current represented company actor
- **WHEN** Delivery independently validates the same original human and exact current accepted company authority
- **THEN** Customer SHALL retain that distinct native actor DATA without inventing a self profile or changing recipient identity
- **AND** the recipient profile, consent, contact and complete typed scope SHALL independently match

### Requirement: Original caller and expiry remain current through completion
Every recipient callback and response SHALL retain the same original authenticated personal caller, bearer, workspace and storefront through owned awaits/disposal. Cancellation and original authority expiry SHALL deny disclosure even if a local receipt has already committed.

#### Scenario: Caller or horizon changes during owner read
- **WHEN** the caller, bearer, workspace, storefront, cancellation or original authority changes before completion
- **THEN** no contact body SHALL be returned and no authority SHALL be renewed

#### Scenario: Replay, revoke and expired consent
- **WHEN** an exact existing access is replayed or its consent is revoked/expired
- **THEN** fresh owner/source/consent checks SHALL precede replay, original expiry SHALL never extend, and revoked/expired access SHALL refuse
